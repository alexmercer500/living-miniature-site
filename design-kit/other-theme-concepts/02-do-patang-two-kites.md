# Theme 2: Do Patang (दो पतंग), Two Kites, One Sky

> *"In a kite fight, when two strings cross, one of them falls. Ours didn't. They tied."*

**Feel:** A colourful, poetic **animated short film** in flat paper-cut style. Bright kites, a sky that changes from dawn to night as you scroll, rooftops, sky lanterns. Modern, joyful and very vibrant, and the **most mobile-friendly** of the three themes because the sky is vertical: scrolling up the phone *is* flying up into the sky.

---

## The story idea

On Uttarayan / Makar Sankranti, the whole country is on its rooftops flying kites. When two kites' strings cross (a *pech*), it's a battle: one string cuts the other, and someone shouts **"Kai po che!"**

This invitation tells the story of **two kites** flown from **two different rooftops** (your two homes / cities / families). The wind (destiny) carries them across the sky. They drift, nearly miss, dance around each other. Then their strings cross, and instead of one cutting the other, **they knot together.** From then on they fly as one.

As the guest scrolls, the **sky moves through a whole day**: dawn (the beginning), bright noon (falling in love), golden sunset (the families joining), and a night full of **sky lanterns** (the wedding celebrations).

**Story arc:** Release the kite → Blessing → Two rooftops, two kites → Drifting together (love story) → The pech (strings cross) → They knot → The families holding the strings → A garland of festival kites (events) → Lantern countdown → Release your own lantern (RSVP + wish) → Night sky with the two kites as stars.

## Visual direction

**Palette:** the sky shifts through four moods; the kites stay vivid throughout.

| Sky time | Gradient | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Dawn | `#FFD6A5` → `#FFB4A2` (peach to rose) | Opening, rooftops |
| Noon | `#8ED1FC` → `#4FC3F7` (bright blue) | Love story |
| Sunset | `#FF9F1C` → `#E5446D` → `#7B2CBF` | Families, Haldi → Sangeet |
| Night | `#2B1B5A` → `#120A2E` (deep indigo) | Wedding, Reception, finale |

| Kite colours | Hex |
|---|---|
| [Bride]'s kite: hot pink | `#FF2E7E` |
| [Groom]'s kite: marigold yellow | `#FFC93C` |
| Event kites | Green `#2EC4B6` · Purple `#7B2CBF` · Red `#E63946` · Orange `#FF7F11` |
| Lantern glow | `#FFB703` |

**Typography (Google Fonts):**
- Display: **Yatra One** (playful, hand-painted Devanagari feel; works for English too)
- Body: **DM Sans** (clean, very readable on phones)
- Handwritten notes / captions: **Kalam** (Indian handwriting style, for messages "written" on kites)

**Art style:** Flat **paper-cut** illustration: layered shapes with soft drop shadows, as if cut from coloured paper. Kites have little tassels (*puchhi*) that flutter. Rooftops have water tanks, clotheslines, potted tulsi and a dog. Small, warm, Indian details.

**Sound:** Wind whoosh + bansuri (flute) melody. A soft **"tring"** each time a kite opens into an event card. A cheer of *"Kai po che!"* (cousins' voices recorded on a phone; very cute) at the knot moment.

## Scroll flow, scene by scene

### Scene 0: Release the Kite (gate screen)
- **See:** A rooftop at dawn, peachy sky. A pink kite sits on the parapet, string wound on a *charkhi* (spool).
- **Interaction:** A round button: **"Hold to let it fly 🪁"**. As the guest presses and holds, a ring fills up (this is the loading progress). On release, the kite **lifts off** and the screen follows it up into the sky. Small toggle below: 🎵 Sound on / off.
- **Why "hold":** it's a tiny ritual that feels like flying a kite, and it covers loading time.

### Scene 1: Shubh Aarambh (the blessing)
- **See:** On the first kite's paper, Ganesh ji painted in simple paper-cut style; marigold tassel.
- **Animation:** The kite gently sways; the shloka writes itself line by line.
- **Copy:**
  > ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥
  > *Before every flight, we seek His blessing for a clear sky.*

### Scene 2: Two Rooftops
- **See:** Split view. **Left:** a rooftop in [City A] with a pink kite rising and a little illustrated [Bride]. **Right:** a rooftop in [City B] with a yellow kite and [Groom]. (On phones they stack: first rooftop, scroll, second rooftop.)
- **Animation:** Each kite climbs as you scroll; a dotted string draws itself from the rooftop to the kite.
- **Copy (handwritten font, like notes):**
  > **[Bride]** · *[City A]* · Flies high, talks a lot, never loses a debate. *(your real quirks here)*
  >
  > **[Groom]** · *[City B]* · Quiet flier, steady hands, secretly very filmy. *(your real quirks here)*

### Scene 3: Drifting (the love story)
- **See:** The open noon sky. The two kites drift closer as the guest scrolls, each **near-miss** being one chapter of your story. Clouds pass by in layers (parallax: far clouds slow, near clouds fast).
- **Animation:** The kites' path is an SVG curve that the scroll "plays" along. Each milestone appears as a **small cloud** with text that the kites fly past.
- **Clouds (fill with your real story):**
  1. ☁️ *"[Year] · The wind first blew them toward each other at [place]."*
  2. ☁️ *"The first conversation: [funny/awkward detail]."*
  3. ☁️ *"They drifted apart for a while. (Every good sky has some clouds.)"* (optional, only if true and you want it)
  4. ☁️ *"[The moment you knew]."*

### Scene 4: The Pech (the signature moment)
- **See:** The screen goes calm, the music drops, and only the two kites remain. Their strings **cross**.
- **Animation:** A dramatic pause: the strings tremble and a tiny "zing" sound plays. The guest expects one to fall. Then the strings **twist around each other and tie into a knot** shaped like a heart. Gulal colour burst. Cousins shout "Kai po che!" (or a drumroll if sound is off).
- **Copy:**
  > *In a kite fight, when two strings cross, one must fall.*
  > *This time, neither did.*
  >
  > **[Bride]** ♥ **[Groom]**
  > *are getting married*
  > **[Date]** · **[City]**

### Scene 5: Who Holds the Strings (the families)
- **See:** The camera follows the now-joined string **down** to the ground, where both families stand together on one rooftop holding the *charkhi*.
- **Copy:**
  > *Every kite flies because someone holds the string.*
  >
  > With the blessings of **Late Shri [Grandfather] & Late Smt. [Grandmother]**
  >
  > **Smt. [Mother] & Shri [Father]** · *parents of [Bride]*
  > **Smt. [Mother] & Shri [Father]** · *parents of [Groom]*
  >
  > *invite you to celebrate with us*

### Scene 6: The Festival of Kites (events)
- **See:** The joined kite flies on, with a **tail of smaller kites** behind it like a garland: one kite per ceremony. As the guest scrolls, each event kite swoops into the centre of the screen and **unfolds into a card** (the kite's diamond shape opens like paper).
- **The sky changes time with each event:**

| Event kite | Sky / colour | What animates | Card line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Haldi** · yellow kite | Morning sun, turmeric yellow | A big sun rises; yellow splash wipe | *"Come in yellow. Leave in more yellow."* · Dress: yellow |
| **Mehendi** · green kite | Late morning, leafy green | Henna vines grow around the card edges (SVG drawing); **easter egg:** names hidden in the vine pattern | *"Find our names hidden in the mehendi."* · Dress: green / floral |
| **Sangeet / Garba** · purple kite | Sunset, magenta & purple | Kites dance in a circle like garba; dandiya sticks clack to the scroll | *"Practice your steps. We'll be judging."* · Dress: chaniya choli / kurta / Indo-western |
| **Baraat** · orange kite | Golden hour | A tiny baraat walks along the rooftops below (horse, dhol, dancing uncles) | *"The groom arrives. The dhol will not stop."* |
| **Wedding** · red & gold kite | Twilight turning to night | The two kites circle a glowing fire point **seven times** (pheras), each loop leaving a gold trail | **Shubh Vivah** · Muhurat: [time] · Dress: traditional |
| **Reception** · silver kite | Full night, stars | Sky lanterns start floating up from the bottom of the screen | *"Dinner, music, and the newlyweds."* · Dress: formal |

Each card has **📍 Directions**, **📅 Add to calendar**, and the dress-code colour swatch.

### Scene 7: Lantern Countdown
- **See:** Night sky; a cluster of **sky lanterns** with numbers glowing on them.
- **Copy:** *"**42** days until two kites become one."*

### Scene 8: Release a Lantern (RSVP + wishes)
- **See:** A paper lantern waiting at the bottom of the screen.
- **Form:** Name · Guests (– / +) · Events attending · **"Write a wish for the couple"**
- **Animation on submit:** The guest's wish is "written" onto the lantern, it lights up, and **floats up** to join all the other lanterns. Optional (needs a small database such as Supabase or Google Sheets): show other guests' lanterns floating in the sky, and tap any one to read its wish. This becomes a beautiful **wishes wall** you can keep forever.

### Scene 9: The Finale (two stars)
- **See:** The night sky fills with everyone's lanterns. The two kites fly up and become **two stars**, then connect into a tiny constellation shaped like your initials.
- **Copy:**
  > *आपकी उपस्थिति हमारा सौभाग्य*
  > *Your presence is our good fortune.*
  >
  > **Swagatotsuk:** [Family names]
  > *Nanhe mehmaan:* "Mere bhaiya ki shaadi mein zaroor aana!" (cousins' names)
  >
  > #[YourHashtag]
- Button: **🪁 Fly again** (back to top)

## Signature interactions
1. **Hold to fly**: the kite takes off from the rooftop
2. **The sky changing from dawn to night** as you scroll (the whole site is one continuous day)
3. **The pech**: strings cross… and tie into a heart
4. **Event kites unfolding into cards**
5. **Releasing your own lantern** with a wish

## Mobile notes
- This theme was designed for phones: one kite in the centre, sky behind, text above and below. Nothing needs swiping.
- The sky gradient is pure CSS (almost zero file size). Kites and clouds are small SVGs. **This will be the lightest and fastest theme**, probably under 3 MB in total.
- On desktop, show both rooftops side by side and give the sky more horizontal space for the kites to drift.
