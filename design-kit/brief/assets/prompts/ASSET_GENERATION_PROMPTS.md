## Consistency guidance
- Keep architecture, lighting, camera height, material style, and palette consistent across all generated scenes.
- Prefer layered, modular elements if the website needs real interactions.
- Do not generate a collage or complete website screenshot as a production asset.
- Generate wide and portrait variants where the composition changes meaningfully.
- Inspect all outputs for malformed architecture, strange text artifacts, or inconsistent cultural details before use.
