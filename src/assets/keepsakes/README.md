# Keepsake photos (optional)

Drop up to three photos here to replace the paintings on the three "Our journey" cards:

| File | Card |
|---|---|
| `1.jpg` | Our roots |
| `2.jpg` | Our paths |
| `3.jpg` | Our tomorrow |

- Any of `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp` or `.avif` works. Use the number as the name.
- A missing number keeps its painting, so you can add one photo at a time.
- The card crops the photo to a short landscape strip, and the tap-to-open frame shows it
  larger. Keep the faces near the centre.
- Use at least 1200 px on the long side. Large phone photos are fine; the build makes small
  AVIF/WebP copies and a blurred loading preview.
- The site is public to anyone with the link, so choose photos you are happy to share.
