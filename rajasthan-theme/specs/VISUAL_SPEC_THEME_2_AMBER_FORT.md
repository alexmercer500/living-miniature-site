# Theme 2 — Amber Fort Romance

## Visual priority
**The fort must be the main focal point.** Show a recognizable, luminous Rajasthan-inspired hill fort in the middle distance, clearly visible against a dramatic sunset. Keep the foreground sparse and architectural. The viewer should immediately notice the fort—not a mass of flowers, furniture, lanterns, rugs, or decorative props.

## Hero composition
- Desktop: 16:9 landscape; large fort occupies the central/middle field; sunset sky provides negative space for real HTML heading; a restrained carved sandstone doorway or columns may frame only the outer edges.
- Mobile: 9:16 portrait composition; keep the fort centered and large enough to read on a phone; preserve sunset sky and title-safe space; avoid simply cropping the desktop image.
- Palette: maroon, amber, terracotta, antique gold, sunset peach, warm ivory, restrained charcoal.
- Foreground decoration: minimal. A few lamps or petals at the lower edge only; no dense flower wall, large sofa, oversized carpet, or many foreground objects.
- Materials: realistic sandstone, carved wood, brass, warm atmospheric light.

## Other scenes
- Entrance: fort visible through an open gateway; sparse edge framing.
- Invitation: isolated ivory envelope with burgundy wax seal and fine gold ornament.
- Story: memory photos/cards in a restrained heritage setting; use supplied personal photos only.
- Celebrations: individual Haldi, Mehndi, Sangeet, Wedding, Reception images, consistent palette and camera.
- Venue/closing: the fort/heritage skyline remains dominant; decoration must not obscure the architecture.

## Interaction continuity
Reuse the current project's scroll choreography, entrance/reveal transitions, event card behavior, and reduced-motion support. The envelope interaction can reuse existing door/reveal logic or add a focused state sequence, but avoid an expensive new renderer unless necessary.
