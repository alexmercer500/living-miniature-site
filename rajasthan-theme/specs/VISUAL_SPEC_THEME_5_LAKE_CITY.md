# Theme 5 — Lake City Elegance

## Visual priority
**The lake and illuminated heritage palace must be the main focal points.** Give the viewer a broad, unobstructed view of the water, palace skyline, mountain/hill silhouette, and sunset-to-blue-hour sky. The lake reflection should be clearly visible and visually important. Flowers, lanterns, and terrace details are only edge framing.

## Hero composition
- Desktop: 16:9 landscape; broad lake fills much of the middle/lower field; illuminated Rajasthan-inspired palace sits clearly across the water; sunset/blue-hour sky provides title-safe negative space.
- Mobile: 9:16 portrait composition; preserve palace, lake reflection, and sky in the composition; do not just crop the desktop art.
- Palette: twilight blue, deep lake teal, champagne gold, dusty rose, warm ivory, navy.
- Foreground decoration: minimal; a few flowers or a single lantern near the outer edges only; no dense floral wall, huge sofa/table grouping, carpets covering the terrace, or excessive diyas.
- Materials: realistic stone, calm reflective water, restrained brass lighting, cinematic sky.

## Other scenes
- Entrance: unobstructed lake-and-palace view through a simple traditional pavilion or stone arch at the edges.
- Invitation: refined ivory scroll or keepsake card with champagne ribbon and subtle gold detail.
- Story: memory cards arranged with generous space and real user-provided photos only.
- Celebrations: individual event vignettes in a coherent blue-hour/champagne palette.
- Venue/closing: broad water reflections and palace lights remain dominant.

## Interaction continuity
Reuse the current project's scroll choreography, reveal transitions, event card behavior, and reduced-motion support. Water shimmer can be subtle and progressive-enhancement only; always retain a static image fallback.
